Automation, growth

Hermes growth loop

Cron-driven agents running the Trillionir and Sodeom growth loop.

Hiring posts→Lead CSV→Targeted email→B2B form
  • Blog agent searches the web for AI news and auto-publishes posts to blogs.thetrillioniar.me.
  • Research agent parses public hiring posts (e.g. Hacker News hiring threads) to extract active engineering leads into a structured CSV.
  • Outreach agent reads that pipeline and delivers targeted 1-to-1 outreach via Resend with strict opt-out headers, directing leads to the B2B form.
  • Video agent turns JS animations into short videos and posts them via Buffer. All agents run on gemma4:31b from Ollama, and an n8n flow mirrors daily commit activity to LinkedIn.
